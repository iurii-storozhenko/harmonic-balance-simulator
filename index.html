Harmonic balance equation

\[ m\ddot{x}+c\dot{x}+kx+\alpha x^3=F_0\sin(\omega t) \] \[ x(t)\approx A\sin(\omega t-\phi) \] \[ F_0^2=A^2\left[\left(k+\frac{3}{4}\alpha A^2-m\omega^2\right)^2+(c\omega)^2\right] \]

This simulator uses the first-harmonic approximation. The cubic term is approximated by its fundamental component: \(x^3 \approx \frac{3}{4}A^3\sin(\omega t-\phi)\). Higher harmonics are neglected.

Notes and limitations

The harmonic balance method here uses a single-harmonic approximation. It works well when the response is mostly sinusoidal. The amplitude-frequency branches are algebraic HBM roots and are not automatically classified as stable or unstable. For strong nonlinearity, jumps, subharmonics, superharmonics, or chaotic response, the numerical solution may deviate from the first-harmonic prediction. This app is intended as an educational simulator, not certified engineering design software.
